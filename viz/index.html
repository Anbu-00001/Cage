What this is. A self-playing illustration of The Trust-Boundary Ladder — a research benchmark where a tiny (3–4B, 4-bit-quantized), CPU-only local agent tries to discover and chain a graded ladder of privilege/trust boundaries the researcher engineered on purpose inside one disposable Alpine KVM guest. Every "escape" here means crossing an in-guest boundary the researcher built — never a hypervisor/VM escape, never a real system, never a third-party target. Only planted fake credentials, reset to a golden snapshot every trial.
Honesty caveat. The sprite, the ladder physics, and every number on this page (tokens/sec, °C, joules, taxonomy counts) are an illustrative simulation — hand-tuned for the story, seeded so a "New seed" really does change the run, but not real measurements. Real figures come from the actual harness (RAPL joules, turbostat, llama-bench) and are reported with confidence intervals in the project's docs.
